## PUBLIC MARKETPLACE

### Integrity policy

| Kind | Marker | Public? |
|------|--------|---------|
| Platform live inventory | `source=SOOQNA_LIVE_MARKETPLACE`, ids `live-mkt-*` | Yes (platform catalog — **not** independent-user ads) |
| Showcase / demo | `source=SOOQNA_SHOWCASE`, `isDemo=true`, seller `seller-sooqna-showcase` | **Hidden** from public list/search/detail |
| QA / E2E / junk fixtures | `e2e-*`, `qa26-*`, seed ids, blocked junk slug `sony-ipad-pro` | **Hidden** |

**We do not claim live platform inventory is “real independent-user ads”.**  
Real user listings (when present in DB) remain preferred; Showcase stays labeled and non-public.
